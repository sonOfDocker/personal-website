Background

I’m a senior full-stack engineer with a platform mindset: I like building reliable, maintainable products end-to-end — from UI and APIs to automation, infrastructure, and the guardrails that help teams ship safely.

AWS Certified Developer – Associate

Most of my experience is in fintech, where consistency, security, and uptime matter. I’ve worked across the SDLC in roles including application development, production support, release and environment management, and incident response — which is where I learned to design for real-world constraints.

Recently I’ve been focused on full-stack delivery of customer- and operations-facing tools: accessible front ends, well-structured APIs, and pragmatic DevOps/CI/CD so delivery stays predictable.

Secondary to platform work, I’m building skills around data-centric systems: ingestion pipelines, normalization, analytics-friendly schemas, and dashboards that turn messy inputs (CSVs/PDFs/reports) into usable insights.

Outside of tech, I’m into sports, music, cooking, and animal rescue.
